# Contributing

For equation or numerical changes, provide the control-volume definition, units, authoritative technical source, assumption/domain changes and tests against independently calculated quantities or published reference cases. Separate correlation data from general algorithms. Do not import manufacturer coefficients without provenance and distribution rights.

For normative changes, identify the official source, edition, scope, access level and applicable jurisdiction. Do not quote copyrighted standards at length or invent clauses. A candidate interface is not a verified mandatory requirement.

For field cases, remove confidential and personal data and clearly distinguish measured, calculated and synthetic information. Include calibration, conditions, uncertainty and permission to publish. Generated images must remain labeled and accompanied by their prompt/provenance.

Before submitting, run self-checks and unittest, regenerate affected outputs, update limitations and explain the observable effect. Actual project release decisions require authorized engineering review outside this repository.
